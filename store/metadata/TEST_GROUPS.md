# Beta test groups

## Apple TestFlight

- Group: `PTT Internal Testers`
- Build: `0.1.0 (1)`
- Test focus: cross-platform encrypted tone transfer, endpoint validation,
  rotation, background return, accessibility, and crash reporting
- Feedback email: the App Store Connect account contact

## Google Play

- Track: Internal testing
- Release: `PTT Talk 0.1.0 (1)`
- Tester list: `PTT Internal Testers`
- Release notes: Initial private technical beta. Tests encrypted prekey setup,
  relay transport, and generated-tone interoperability with iOS.

Tester membership is intentionally not inferred from other apps. Add only
people who have explicitly agreed to participate in this PTT beta.
